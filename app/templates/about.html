About Interior Design AI

Transforming spaces with artificial intelligence

What We Do

Interior Design AI is a cutting-edge web application that uses OpenAI's advanced image editing technology to transform your living spaces. Simply upload a photo of any room, and our AI will redesign it according to your style preferences.

How It Works

  1. Upload: Take a photo of your room and upload it to our platform
  2. Customize: Choose your preferred room type, design style, color palette, and furniture options
  3. Generate: Our AI processes your image and preferences to create a stunning redesign
  4. Download: Get your professionally designed room image in high quality

Technology

Built with Flask and powered by OpenAI's DALL-E image editing API, our platform delivers photorealistic interior design transformations that maintain the structural integrity of your space while completely reimagining its aesthetic appeal.
